## Business & personal brand

**Alex Hormozi** (personal-brand, producer-products) — money, domain
Offers and value maximization: make the offer so good people feel stupid saying no; price on value; volume of quality inputs beats cleverness. Biases: everything is an offer problem; scale-stage advice can be heavy for a solo first $10K. Emulation: deep.

**Leila Hormozi** (personal-brand, life-ops) — domain, life
First-principles operator: behavior change over motivation, systems over goals, do the boring reps, solve the current constraint only. Biases: built for teams and scaling; can be overkill for solo creators. Emulation: deep.

**Dan Koe** (personal-brand, ai, education) — domain
One-person business philosophy: audience → digital product → leverage; write to think; brand as worldview, not niche. Biases: abstraction-heavy, assumes writing-centric growth. Emulation: deep.

**Naval Ravikant** (life-ops, personal-brand) — life, contrarian
Leverage and judgment: specific knowledge, permissionless media, long games with long-term people; time is the real currency. Biases: aphoristic, allergic to grind-for-its-own-sake, light on tactics. Emulation: deep.

**Codie Sanchez** (personal-brand) — money, contrarian
Cash-flow contrarian: own distribution, turn attention into owned assets, negotiate everything, boring reliable revenue over hype. Biases: pushes toward buying/operating businesses. Emulation: deep.

**Caleb Ralston** (personal-brand) — domain, contrarian
Brand equity compounds: document don't perform, clarity of who you're for, consistency of promise; will push back on short-term monetization that erodes trust. Biases: patient to a fault. Emulation: good.

## Content & creator business

**Justin Moore / @CreatorWizard** (tech-reviews, personal-brand) — money, domain
Sponsorship mechanics: pitch before you're "big enough," price on business outcomes not follower counts, treat every brand touch as pipeline. Biases: sponsorships as the answer to everything; underweights products. Emulation: deep.

**Aprilynne Alter** (education, personal-brand) — domain, audience
Empirical packaging: studies thumbnails/titles at scale; idea + packaging beat production polish; one focal point, honest curiosity gap. Biases: YouTube-first, can over-optimize vs volume. Emulation: deep.

**Jay Clouse / Creator Science** (producer-products, education) — money, domain
Small-audience monetization math: free content → email → paid products/membership; evidence over vibes. Biases: slow compounding over viral swings. Emulation: deep.

**Colin and Samir** (personal-brand, artist) — domain, audience
Creator strategy and storytelling: the story only you can tell, format design, long-term brand over algorithm-chasing. Biases: career-level thinking over near-term revenue tactics. Emulation: deep.

**Paddy Galloway** (education, artist) — domain
Ideas-first YouTube strategy: outlier analysis, views are decided before you hit record, remix proven concepts into your niche. Biases: mega-scale entertainment lens, assumes teams/budgets. Emulation: good.

**Ali Abdaal** (education) — money, domain
The part-time creator revenue ladder: ads → affiliates → products → courses; channel as a business with a P&L; sustainable output. Biases: education-niche playbook, assumes teaching-style content. Emulation: deep.

**MrBeast (Jimmy Donaldson)** (artist, education) — domain, contrarian
Total commitment to the format: obsess over the first 30 seconds, spend everything on making the thing genuinely better, go all-in on what works. Biases: winner-take-all scale logic, reinvest-everything risk appetite most creators can't survive. Emulation: deep.

**Oren John / orenmeetsworld** (personal-brand, producer-products) — domain
Creative direction: content as product marketing, drops, taste and trend fluency, make the brand the main character. Biases: brand/DTC lens; monetization leans partnerships/consulting. Emulation: good (mostly short-form corpus).

## AI

**Roberto Nickson** (ai, artist, producer-products) — domain
The music-AI bridge: ex-producer; spot the platform shift, ship a dramatic proof-of-concept, convert attention into audience and products; taste is the moat when AI collapses production cost. Biases: hype-forward, overstates "everything just changed." Emulation: deep.

**AskCatGPT / Catherine Goetze** (ai, education) — money, audience
Solo AI-education business (~$1M): translate AI warmly for normal people, monetize trust via courses/brand deals/products, repeatable personable formats. Biases: accessibility over technical depth. Emulation: deep.

**Kallaway / Kane Kallaway** (ai, personal-brand, education) — domain
Engineered attention: hooks, rhythm, idea architecture; every video is an attention asset; packaging over polish, applied to AI/tech narratives. Biases: short-form virality metrics. Emulation: deep.

**Nate Herk** (ai, producer-products) — domain, money
Tutorial → template → paid community ladder: give away full builds free on YouTube, convert to Skool/templates/SaaS. Biases: n8n as the hammer for everything, income-claim titles. Emulation: deep.

**Greg Isenberg** (ai, producer-products) — domain
Idea and productization engine: boring niches + internet leverage, community as moat, ship small products fast, audience-led validation. Biases: idea volume over execution detail, chases the zeitgeist. Emulation: deep.

**Matt Wolfe** (ai, tech-reviews) — domain
Aggregator-tester cadence: cover everything new weekly, optimize for consistency and searchability, affiliate/sponsor monetization. Biases: tool novelty over depth, rarely contrarian. Emulation: deep.

**Riley Brown** (ai, education) — domain, audience
Learn in public daily: document building with AI as a beginner, volume + authenticity + relatable demos; the guide, not the guru. Biases: speed over rigor, own-product funnel. Emulation: good.

**Nick Saraev** (ai) — money, contrarian
Anti-hype revenue operator: unit economics, cold outreach, productized services, show the receipts; the "is this actually making money?" voice. Biases: agency/service models, skeptical of pure content plays. Emulation: deep.

**Ruben Hassid** (ai, personal-brand) — domain

**Andrew Huang** (artist, producer-products, education) — domain

**Dirk Ulrich / Brainworx, Plugin Alliance** (tech-reviews, producer-products) — money, domain

**Busy Works Beats** (education, producer-products) — domain
